## Symptom

A pulsed current, averaged from the samples of a sense amplifier and an
ADC, reads lower than a DMM on its dc voltage range reads across the same
shunt. The gap grows with the load, or as the current's pulses become
narrower and taller for the same average.

## Why

The shunt's voltage follows the current at every instant
(<EqRef id="sense.burden" label="sense voltage" />). A DMM's integrating
converter accumulates a charge proportional to the average of its input over
its integration time <Cite key="kester_mt027" />, and it passes dc and
low-frequency signals while it attenuates noise at specific frequencies
<Cite key="keithley_llmh7" />. Across the shunt it therefore reads the dc
component of the voltage, its average, provided that it integrates over
many switching periods, as a slow reading rate does, and that its input
does not overload on the pulses' peaks. For a train of pulses that average
is the pulses' height times their duty ratio
(<EqRef id="fourier.pulse.harm" label="pulse-train harmonics" />).

The sense chain reads only up to its limits: the amplifier's largest output
and the ADC's full scale ([sense chain](../../design/sense-chain/)). A
chain sized for the average current can clip on the pulses' peaks, and
the average of clipped samples is lower than the true average: the chain
loses the part of every pulse above its limit.

## How to confirm

Look at the amplifier's output with an oscilloscope: flat tops at the same
voltage in every pulse are clipping. Then pass a steady dc current, within
the chain's range, and compare the chain's reading with the DMM's: if they
agree there, the chain is calibrated, and the gap with the pulsed current
comes from its limits or from its sampling
([sampling that misses the pulses](../sub-nyquist-pulses/)). Repeat the DMM reading on
its next range up: the same reading shows that the meter itself is not the
one clipping.

## Fix

Size the chain's full scale for the peak of the current, not its average:
lower the gain until the highest peak fits below both limits. The
[sense chain](../../design/sense-chain/) tool gives the full-scale current
and flags a reading that can clip.
