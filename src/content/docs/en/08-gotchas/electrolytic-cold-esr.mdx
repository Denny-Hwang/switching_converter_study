## Symptom

At the cold end of its temperature range a converter's output ripple grows
several times over, while everything behaves at room temperature.

## Why

An aluminum electrolytic capacitor's ESR can increase more than tenfold at
its low-temperature limit <Cite key="cde_ae_guide" />. The ripple current
through the capacitor makes a ripple voltage across the ESR in proportion,
which adds to the capacitive ripple

## How to confirm

Measure the output ripple, or the capacitor's ESR at the switching
frequency, with the capacitor at the lowest temperature of the
specification, in a climatic chamber, and compare it with the value at room
temperature.

## Fix

Size the capacitor with its ESR at the lowest temperature, from its data
sheet, so that the ripple meets the limit there. Capacitors in parallel
share the ripple current and divide the ESR, so more of them lower the
ripple as well.
