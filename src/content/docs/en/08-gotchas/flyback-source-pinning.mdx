## Symptom

A flyback sized to present a high-impedance source's resistance takes the
expected share of the source's power at small amplitudes. Above some
amplitude its input voltage stops following the source, and it takes less
than the source's available power.

## Why

In DCM a fixed-duty-ratio flyback presents a resistance at its input
(<EqRef id="lfr.R_in" label="loss-free resistor" />)
<Cite key="erickson2020" where="Ch. 15 (AC and DC Equivalent Circuit Modeling of the Discontinuous Conduction Mode)" />.
It stays in DCM only below the
<EqRef id="flyback.V_crit" label="critical input voltage" />. A source strong
enough to raise the input to it drives the flyback into CCM, where, with the
output fixed, the conversion ratio allows only that one input voltage
<Cite key="erickson2020" where="Ch. 6 (Converter Circuits)" />. The input stays
there: the flyback has become a constant-voltage sink, and its share of the
source's power depends on the source's amplitude
(<EqRef id="src.cv_extraction" label="constant-voltage extraction" />).

In the example, a source of <Val example="flyback-source" name="V_oc" />
behind <Val example="flyback-source" name="R_s" /> can give
<Val example="flyback-source" name="P_max" /> to a matched resistance. The
flyback's input is held at its critical voltage,
<Val example="flyback-source" name="V_gcrit" />, where it takes a share of
<Val example="flyback-source" name="eta_ext" /> of that power.

## How to confirm

Plot the input voltage against the source's amplitude: it follows the
amplitude in DCM and flattens at the critical voltage. Or watch the
magnetizing current: in CCM it no longer falls to zero in each period. The
simulator's current-limited source shows the input settling at the critical
voltage:

<TrySim example="flyback-source" topology="flyback" />

## Fix

Choose the duty ratio and the turns ratio so that the critical voltage lies
above the highest input voltage the source can drive
(<EqRef id="lfr.Vg" label="bus voltage of a loss-free resistor" />), then
rate the switch for the voltage that follows
(<EqRef id="flyback.Vds_off" label="switch voltage" />). A smaller turns
ratio raises the critical voltage.
