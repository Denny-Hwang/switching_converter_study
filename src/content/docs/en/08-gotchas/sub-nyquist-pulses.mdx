## Symptom

The average of ADC samples of a pulsed current, such as a switch's current
or an inductor's current in DCM, jumps or drifts when the switching
frequency or the sampling rate changes slightly, sits far from a DMM's
reading, or reads zero at light load, although the ADC samples at ten times
the switching frequency.

## Why

A sampled signal represents only the band from dc to half the sampling
rate (<EqRef id="adc.nyquist" label="Nyquist frequency" />); any component
above it aliases back into that band <Cite key="kester_mt002" />. A pulse
train has harmonics at every multiple of the switching frequency, and they
fall only in inverse proportion to their order
(<EqRef id="fourier.pulse.harm" label="pulse-train harmonics" />): the
narrower the pulses, the more of them stay large. Sampling at ten times the
switching frequency puts the Nyquist frequency at five times it: every
harmonic above the fifth aliases. The tenth, the twentieth and every other
harmonic at a multiple of the sampling rate fold onto dc, the average
itself, and a harmonic near such a multiple folds to a slow beat: the
average drifts.

Seen in time: when the samples lie further apart than a pulse is wide, a
sample falls inside a pulse only by chance. With the sampling rate an exact
multiple of the switching frequency, the samples fall at the same points of
every period, and read the pulse either every time or never.

## How to confirm

Change the sampling rate by a small fraction and watch the average: a
reading that moves is aliasing. Compare it with an average taken before the
sampling, such as a DMM across the shunt
([a current reading below the meter](../dmm-true-average/)).

## Fix

Filter before the ADC: an anti-aliasing filter attenuates the components
above the Nyquist frequency <Cite key="kester_mt002" />, and for the average
current its corner belongs well below the switching frequency. The
[sense chain](../../design/sense-chain/) tool checks the corner against the
Nyquist frequency and gives the filter's gain at the switching frequency.
Alternatively, sample asynchronously to the switching and average over many
periods, or take many samples inside the narrowest pulse: at a rate locked
to the switching frequency, n samples per pulse still misread its area by
up to one n-th.
