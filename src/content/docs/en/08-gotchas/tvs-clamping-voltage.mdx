## Symptom

A flyback's switch fails, or its drain voltage at turn-off peaks above the
value the primary clamp was chosen for, although the TVS's breakdown voltage
left margin to the switch's rating.

## Why

A TVS begins to conduct at its breakdown voltage, but while it carries a
current it holds its clamping voltage: the breakdown voltage plus its
dynamic resistance times the current <Cite key="st_an316" />
(<EqRef id="tvs.V_clamp" label="clamping voltage" />). The data sheet's
clamping voltage is a maximum at a standard pulse's peak current; the
dynamic resistance depends on the pulse's duration, and the breakdown
voltage rises with temperature <Cite key="st_an316" />. In a flyback the
clamp takes the leakage inductance's current at turn-off, and the switch
sees the input voltage plus the clamp's voltage
(<EqRef id="clamp.Vds" label="switch voltage while the clamp conducts" />).

In the clamp tool's example, a TVS that breaks down at
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="V_BR" /> and clamps at
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="V_CL" /> at its
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="I_PP" /> pulse has a dynamic resistance of
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="R_D" />. At the flyback's
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="I_pk" /> it holds
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="V_clamp" />, and the switch sees
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="V_DS" /> against its
<Val example="clamp-tvs" name="V_rating" /> rating.

## How to confirm

Capture the drain voltage at turn-off, with a probe rated for it and a short
ground connection ([probes](../../06-bench/probes/)): while the clamp
conducts, the drain sits at the input voltage plus the clamp's voltage.
Compare that plateau with the clamping voltage at the actual peak current,
and its peak with the switch's rating.

## Fix

Choose the TVS by its clamping voltage at the actual peak current and at
its highest temperature, with margin to the switch's rating; the
[clamp check](../../design/clamp-check/) computes it from the data sheet's
breakdown and clamping voltages. Keep the clamp's voltage well above the
reflected output voltage as well: a clamp voltage close to it takes much
more than the leakage energy <Cite key="ti_ssztcv6" />.
