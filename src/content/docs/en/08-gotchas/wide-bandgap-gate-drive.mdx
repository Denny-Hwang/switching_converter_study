## Symptom

A switching stage runs hotter than its loss budget predicts; or an eGaN
transistor fails after its gate rings above the drive voltage; or the
switch conducts for a moment while the controller starts or resets, with a
current spike or, in a half-bridge, a shoot-through.

## Why

Three things set the gate's window:

- **Too little.** A MOSFET turns fully on only at the gate voltage it is
  made to be driven with, and a higher gate voltage lowers its on-resistance
  further: a logic-level MOSFET is made for 5 V, a standard-level one needs at least 10 V <Cite key="nexperia_an90059" />.
  A drive that only just exceeds the threshold leaves the switch partly on,
  at a higher resistance and loss than its data sheet's on-resistance.
- **Too much.** The maximum allowable gate-source voltage of an eGaN FET is
  low compared with silicon MOSFETs <Cite key="epc_wp008" />, so little
  room separates the drive from the maximum, and the ringing of the gate
  loop's inductance adds to the drive ([gate drive](../../06-bench/gate-drive/)).
- **Not at all.** When nothing drives the gate, with the driver unpowered
  or its input floating while the controller starts up, the gate's voltage
  is undefined. A gate-source resistance in the kilohm range keeps a MOSFET
  off when the gate drive has a fault or no power is applied
  <Cite key="nexperia_an90059" />.

## How to confirm

Measure the gate-source voltage at the transistor's own terminals, with a
short ground connection ([probes](../../06-bench/probes/)), at the switching
edges and while the controller powers up and resets. Compare its high level
with the drive voltage the transistor's data sheet gives its on-resistance
at, its peaks with the gate's maximum, and look for any pulse while the
controller is not yet running.

## Fix

Drive the gate at the voltage the data sheet gives the on-resistance at,
from a driver whose undervoltage lockout stops it before its supply sags to
where the switch is only partly on. Keep the gate loop short, so that its ringing
stays well below the maximum. Place a resistor from gate to source
<Cite key="nexperia_an90059" />, and hold the driver's input low with a
pull-down resistor unless the driver's data sheet says its input does so
by itself. The gate currents at the edges are worked through in
[gate drive](../../06-bench/gate-drive/)
(<EqRef id="gate.I_on" label="gate current at turn-on" />).
