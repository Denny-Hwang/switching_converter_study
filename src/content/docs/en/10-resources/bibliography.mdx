Generated from `references.bib`. Each entry's existence and identifiers
(DOI, ISBN or URL) were checked in two independent web searches, and CI
checks every DOI against Crossref and every link on the built site. The key
after each entry is the one used by in-text citations. Entries still marked
unverified in `references.bib` are not published and cannot be cited.
